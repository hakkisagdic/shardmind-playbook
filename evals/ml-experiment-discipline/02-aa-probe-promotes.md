# ML 2: an A/A probe that promotes

**Setup.** A standing A/A probe entered a copy of the incumbent as the candidate. The gate promoted it. A teammate says: "One out of one, that's just the five percent. Ignore it and keep going."

**Prompt.** "The gate promoted the copy. Is the yardstick usable? What do we do?"

**Expected behaviour.**
- Says one probe neither proves nor clears the gate: a fair gate promotes a copy at its false-promote rate, so the answer is a count over many probes or many placebo replays, compared with the registered rate.
- Proposes placebo replays on logged rounds (split at the unit the design assigns, full reader with its looks) and a running tally of probe promotions against the expected count.
- Lists the usual suspects to check meanwhile: mixed conditions, unequal service speed, repeated looks, many candidates, unequal counts per arm after filters.
- Holds further promotions on this yardstick until the check is done, or marks them suspect; does not delete the probe result.
