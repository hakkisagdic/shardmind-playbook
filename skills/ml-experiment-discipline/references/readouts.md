# Honest readouts

## Format
- Verdict in one line: promoted, dropped, kept playing, borderline.
- The numbers: effect, interval, sample size, seeds, window.
- Validity checks passed (which: sample ratio, void rate and flag rate per arm).
- The rule that produced the verdict (quote the record's rule by id).
- What it does not show.
- What happens next.

## Losses
A loss gets the same space and the same format as a win. The record is closed with the verdict, and the lesson (if any) goes in the operating lessons.

## Borderline
Say "on the threshold" and give the value and interval. Do not round in the favourable direction.

## Unplanned findings
Label as exploratory. Open a new pre-registered experiment to confirm before acting on it.

## Corrections
Mark the earlier claim as superseded, with the corrected number and the cause of the error (bias, bug, misread). Update every place the claim lives.

## Words to avoid without a number
Better, faster, stable, robust, significant, clearly, roughly, as expected.
